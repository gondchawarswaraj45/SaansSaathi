1. EXECUTIVE PITCH (THE 90-SECOND ELEVATOR PITCH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Every winter, 300 million citizens across Northern India are trapped under a
lethal ceiling of toxic smog caused by atmospheric temperature inversion. AQI
numbers hit 400 to 500+, yet parents, senior citizens, and patients are left
with single, uncalibrated numbers that offer zero guidance on when it is safe to
step outside or how to protect themselves indoors.

SaansSaathi (सांस साथी - 'Your Breath Companion') is an end-to-end, full-stack
AI air quality and family health protection platform engineered specifically
for Indian urban conditions.

Unlike generic weather apps that merely show a red number, SaansSaathi:
1. Calculates real-time CPCB National AQI using exact linear interpolation across
   8 pollutants.
2. Synchronizes daily routines to atmospheric inversion curves—alerting families
   to avoid the 6:00-10:00 AM smog trap and leverage the 1:00-3:30 PM golden
   ventilation window.
3. Profiles demographic vulnerabilities (infants, asthmatics, seniors, pregnant
   women) with clinical safety thresholds.
4. Bridges the indoor gap with EPA Clean Air Room blueprints and an affordable
   ₹1,500 DIY Box-Fan HEPA Purifier schematic.
5. Deploys Google Gemini 1.5 Flash with strict medical triage boundaries and
   offline NLP fallback.

SaansSaathi transforms paralyzing environmental anxiety into structured,
verifiable, and affordable family protection."

3. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY (15 STEPS DELIVERED)
================================================================================

Step 1: Scaffolding
- Spring Boot 3.3.6 (Java 17) + React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS v3.
- Modular hexagonal architecture with controllers, services, repositories.

Step 2: Authentication & JWT
- Stateless HMAC-256 JWT security with 1-hour access tokens.
- Rotating refresh tokens stored in DB with 7-day expiration.
- BCrypt password hashing (strength 12) and secure `/api/auth/me`.

Step 3: AQI Telemetry Engine
- CPCB piecewise linear interpolation formula:
  I = [(I_high - I_low) / (B_high - B_low)] * (C - B_low) + I_low.
- Telemetry integration for PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb.
- Open-Meteo inversion, temperature, and wind speed correlation.
- Recharts 24-hour diurnal curve with inversion peak indicators.

Step 4: Family Vulnerability Management
- Household profiling for Infants, Children, Adults, and Seniors.
- Clinical sensitivity triggers: Asthma, COPD, Cardiovascular, Pregnancy.
- Automatic risk tier evaluation: Low, Moderate, High, Severe.

Step 5: Indoor Air Quality Assistant
- 3-step home assessment wizard calculating indoor safety scores (0-100).
- EPA Clean Air Room blueprint for room volume sealing.
- Detailed schematic for ₹1,500 DIY Box-Fan + MERV-13 air purifier.

Step 6: AI Saathi Copilot (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash)
- Prompt context enrichment: live station AQI + family sensitivities + home score.
- Strict medical triage guardrails: never prescribes or diagnoses; provides 112
  emergency hotline advice for acute respiratory distress.
- Offline NLP fallback engine for zero-downtime resilience.

Step 7: Daily Routine Planner
- Diurnal routine engine scheduling activities around boundary layer heights.
- Automatic shifts for morning jogging and school transit.
- Task completion tracking and 7-day compliance history.

Step 8: Interactive Geospatial AQI Map
- Leaflet + OpenStreetMap + CartoDB dark/light tile basemaps.
- Custom CPCB color-coded station pin badges with radial radar pulse.
- One-click "Set as My Location" and GPS Locate Me browser navigation.

Step 9: Alerts & Notifications System
- Spring background scheduler (`AqiAlertJob` every 30 mins) with anti-spam rules.
- 7:00 AM proactive morning briefing push delivery.
- Real-time unread badge counter in top navbar bell.

Step 10: Clean Air Community Challenges
- 10 structured environmental challenges with points and duration.
- 6 unlockable achievement badges.
- 7-day progress logging calendar.

Step 11: Landing & Legal Transparency
- Public unauthenticated live smog ticker and 3-pillar feature breakdown.
- 8-item interactive FAQ accordion.
- Comprehensive About, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service pages.

Step 12: Admin & Telemetry Dashboard
- Live monitoring of upstream API health (OpenAQ, Open-Meteo, Gemini, SQLite).
- User management table with active status toggle.
- Emergency smog broadcast modal with instant alert dispatch.

Step 13: Testing & Quality Assurance
- Backend: 36 JUnit 5 / MockMvc tests passing (100% clean build).
- Frontend: 12 Vitest / React Testing Library tests passing.
- Mobile responsive sign-off across 375px, 768px, 1280px viewports.

Step 14: Deployment & CI/CD Pipelines
- Multi-stage Dockerfile (Eclipse Temurin 17 JRE) with `/data` persistent mount.
- `render.yaml` 1-click cloud infrastructure blueprint.
- `vercel.json` SPA routing rewrite rules and security headers.
- GitHub Actions automated pipelines (`backend-ci.yml`, `frontend-ci.yml`).

Step 15: Documentation & Final Polish
- Exhaustive documentation suite: README.md, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md,
  docs/DATABASE.md, docs/API.md, docs/DESIGN.md, docs/postman-collection.json.

- Verified medical disclaimers and data attributions (CPCB, OpenAQ, Open-Meteo).
